- Findings that change how these clips can be read:
  - The tracker-gate labels on the five gate clips (for example "23M") are the gate's **width in metres**, not a range: the label follows the gate size when the gate resizes at fixed zoom (PR044 slope 1.08, PR059 slope 0.99; PR052 by eye).
  - PR111's label behaves like **slant range** to a level plane, not a gate size, which gives heights below the sensor for two objects.
  - PR113's burned-in scale is a **HUD pitch ladder**, not a depression scale. If conformal, the horizontal field of view is about 39 deg (28 deg by a second, rougher pixel-aspect indicator), not the 54 deg Haqq-Misra and Kopparapu assumed, and the object's angular rate is 12 to 21 percent lower.
  - PR149 reproduces Haqq-Misra and Kopparapu's bound: 98 to 130 m/s across the line of sight for a 150 to 200 m ship at the ship's range (theirs 99 to 132), and the ratio-1 value is a true maximum for an object above the sea.

## What the results say (plain words)

No PURSUE clip can be reconstructed in 3D: the numbers that would fix the line of sight and the range are redacted. What remains are sizes and speeds as functions of an unknown range. The tracker labels, read correctly as gate widths, give the object's size and cross-range speed at the range the tracker used (for example PR044: a 0.8 to 1.1 m object moving about 1 to 14 m/s across the line of sight at the gate's range); off that range both scale in proportion. The two clips the 2026 paper analyzed hold up in part: PR149's speed bound is reproduced, while PR113's angular scale is a pitch ladder and implies a narrower field of view and a slower angular rate than the paper used.

## Known issues / debt

- PR113's pixel aspect: two indicators disagree (1.31 from the ladder fit over rolls up to 14 deg, about 1.8 from the round reticle by eye).
- The field of view of the gate clips' sensors is unpublished, so the gate's own range is a curve over the assumed field of view.
- PR078 and PR089 never resize their gate at fixed zoom, so the width reading is applied to them from the other gate clips, not tested on them.
- Speeds are the component across the line of sight, relative to the platform: lower bounds on the full relative speed.
