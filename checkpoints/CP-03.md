## What this means so far (plain words)

- **GOFAST:** the object is at about 13,000 ft, not near the sea, as both Sitrec and AARO found; the lab reproduces Sitrec within 41 ft and 1 kt. Its speed through the air depends on the wind at that height (next: ERA5).
- **GIMBAL:** the video's geometry allows both readings. At the aircrew's 6 to 8 nmi the object must slow, stop and reverse; near 32 nmi a steady, straight, level flight at about 260 to 295 kt over the ground fits. The lab's own cloud measurement removed the main technical objection to the distant reading (with the bank-derived turn rate the distant path had no solution on many frames). Which reading is right is a question for the discriminators and the testimony in M3, not for the video geometry alone.
