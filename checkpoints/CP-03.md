## What this means so far (plain words)

- **GOFAST:** the object is at about 13,000 ft, not near the sea, as both Sitrec and AARO found; the lab reproduces Sitrec within 41 ft and 1 kt. Its speed through the air depends on the wind at that height (next: ERA5).
- **GIMBAL:** the video's geometry allows both readings, under conditions. At the aircrew's 6 to 8 nmi the object must slow, stop and reverse. Near 32 nmi a straight, level path at about 260 to 295 kt over the ground fits, though its air heading wanders by 26 to 42 deg, so it is not a perfectly steady flight; and it exists only if the turn rate follows the cloud motion and the field of view is 0.35 deg (at 0.7 deg it fails). Which reading is right is a question for the discriminators and the testimony in M3, not for the video geometry alone; the field of view is itself one of the discriminating unknowns.
