Date: 10/31/2026
Milestone: M1

## Accomplished

- Every on-screen readout of GIMBAL and GOFAST read on every frame (1031 each) with a confidence per value: azimuth, elevation, range and closure rate (GOFAST), altitude, airspeed, Mach, clock, zoom, sensor, polarity (`extract/symbology.py`, glyph templates labeled by eye).
- Interlaced transition frames (the display changed between the two fields of one frame) flagged with both values (`extract/transitions.py`).
- Image cues per frame: bank from the horizon bars, the object's position, size, orientation and brightness, and background motion (`extract/image_cues.py`).
- A manual correction tool and a merge step; the solver reads only `symbology_final.csv`.
- Two independent blind checks.

Caveats on that result: it is a single sample of 100 frames per video, and GIMBAL azimuth sits exactly at the limit (1 error in 100 is statistically consistent with a true rate of up to about 5 percent). The transition flags did not affect round 2: every round-2 cell passes on the reader's own single value. The transition detector missed 2 of the 5 two-value GIMBAL azimuth cells the round-2 verifier marked (f956, f1010), so the flags are incomplete and are not relied on for any error rate.

Round 1 is kept for the record: raw 8/1800 (GIMBAL azimuth 3/100 and airspeed 2/100, GOFAST clock 2/100 and altitude 1/100). Of those, 2 were the verifier's misreads, 5 were interlaced two-value frames and 1 (GIMBAL f194 azimuth) was a clear reader error. Round-1 rates are quoted raw only; a with-flags rate would hide the f194 error, because the detector also flags that frame. The first independent review (`artifacts/review/CP-01-02-review.md`) judged round 1 a Fail against the target and objected that the transition detector was checked on the same frames it was built for; round 2 answers that with fresh frames and a fresh verifier who marked two-value cells independently (5 in GIMBAL, 8 in GOFAST, all matched by the reader's value or its flags).

Agreement with Sitrec's independently extracted tables (`data/derived/<case>/symbology_sitrec_check.json`): GIMBAL azimuth 97.3 percent over 1030 frames at an offset of -1 frame (all differences 1 deg, at change frames); GOFAST azimuth 99.0 percent (offset +1), elevation 99.4 percent, range 99.25 percent.

Measured display cadence: readouts update on a 6-frame cycle (about 5 Hz), not every other frame as the 2023 paper states (`docs/prior-work/FINDINGS-LOG.md`).

Tests: `tools/gate.py` (81 tests, skips detected via a JUnit report). The intermittent browser test is fixed (it now waits for the image, focus and typed value; 17 of the 18 runs after the first fix passed, and runs after the second fix pass). One server API test timed out once while the machine was heavily loaded by parallel agents; three quiet reruns pass (see Known issues).

Independent check: a fresh verifier reviewed round 2 (`artifacts/review/CP-02-review.md`): sample, scoring, image spot checks (30 cells, all matched) and the gate passed; it asked for the tag, the limit caveat and the detector misses to be stated, which is done above.
