## Verdicts

- **GOFAST:** reconstructable to tight bounds (range, angles, altitude, speed and a closure rate on screen).
- **GIMBAL:** line of sight reconstructable; range not determined (results as curves over range).
- **FLIR1:** line of sight with lower confidence; range not determined.
- **PURSUE:** none fully reconstructable; 17 clips bounded (tracker-gate size labels, tick-marked reticles, an angle ladder, partial pod angles, or known-size objects), 117 not viable.

## Findings worth knowing now (`docs/prior-work/FINDINGS-LOG.md`)

- GIMBAL true airspeed is about 352 to 360 kt (from both IAS and the on-screen Mach), not the 2023 paper's 363 to 369 kt; Sitrec uses 351.
- GOFAST shows a closure rate (Vc) frame by frame that is not among Sitrec's or AARO's inputs; it agrees with the change in range (about 177 kt average).
- PR113's scale ladder rolls with the picture and reverses direction, so it may be a pitch ladder rather than the depression-angle scale assumed by Haqq-Misra and Kopparapu.
